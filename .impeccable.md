## Design Context

### Users
A personal Arabic letter for Tota, primarily read on a phone. The sender wants to thank her for her patience with a difficult mobile-phone situation, acknowledge its impact on her, and promise better time management and presence at home.

### Brand Personality
Tender, sincere, intimate. Egyptian Arabic, with gratitude and personal responsibility, never pressure to forgive.

### Aesthetic Direction
User confirmed: cream paper, rose details and an intimate letter feeling. Updated direction: rich scroll-driven website animation, with interactive opening, page navigation and return actions. Tapping the wax seal starts the SoundCloud song and plays synthesized sound effects (seal crack, paper rustle, whoosh, sparkle, page turn) in sync with the opening; scrolling still drives and reverses the motion. The envelope opens and its paper becomes the letter; promises use separate drawn illustrations. Hero decoration: floating petals, soft aurora glow, bokeh, hand-drawn rose branches.

### Design Principles
- Let the words carry the emotion; decoration supports reading.
- Keep all essential content accessible in Arabic and RTL.
- Make the letter opening a meaningful interaction.
- Make motion follow scroll and deliberate clicks, reverse naturally when scrolling backward, and honor reduced-motion preferences.
- Express promises through concrete changes in behavior.
